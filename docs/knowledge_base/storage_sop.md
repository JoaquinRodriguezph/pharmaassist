# Storage Standard Operating Procedure

Document ID: SOP-STO-001. Applies to: all branches.

## Purpose And Scope

This standard operating procedure describes how medicines and health products are received, stored and monitored so that they stay safe and effective until they are dispensed or sold. It covers the shop floor, the stockroom, the backroom and the cold-chain refrigerator at every branch. All staff who receive deliveries, shelve stock or take temperature readings must read this procedure before they start those tasks, and must follow it exactly. Any step that cannot be completed as written must be reported to the supervising pharmacist on duty before the work continues.

## Temperature Monitoring

Ambient store and stockroom temperature must be maintained within 15°C–25°C.

1. Staff must check and record temperatures twice daily, at 8:00 AM and at 4:00 PM.
2. Each reading is written on the room temperature log sheet with the time, the temperature and the initials of the person who took it.
3. A reading outside 15°C–25°C must be reported to the supervising pharmacist on the same day, and the action taken is written beside the reading.
4. The cold-chain refrigerator is read at the same two times, and its range and rules are set out in the section on cold-chain storage and excursions.

Log sheets are kept at the branch and must not be altered after the day on which they are completed.

## Cold Chain And Excursions

Cold-chain products, including vaccines, must be stored in the designated refrigerator. The acceptable cold-chain storage range is 2°C–8°C. An excursion is any period in which the recorded temperature is outside 2°C–8°C.

For any excursion lasting longer than 30 minutes, staff must:

1. Tag all affected stock "DO NOT DISPENSE - QUARANTINE" and keep it in the refrigerator.
2. Record the peak temperature and the duration of the excursion in the temperature log.
3. Notify the supervising pharmacist before any unit is sold or dispensed.

Do not discard affected stock. The supervising pharmacist decides whether it can be released or must be returned or destroyed. An excursion of 30 minutes or less is still written in the temperature log.

## Receiving And FEFO

Every delivery is inspected at the time it arrives, before it is put away.

1. Check the delivery against the order for product, quantity and batch number.
2. Check each package for damage, and check the expiry date of every batch. Reject any item that is damaged or already expired.
3. Cold-chain items go into the refrigerator immediately.
4. Store ambient products at 15°C–25°C.

Shelve stock using FEFO (First-Expired, First-Out). Place batches with the earliest expiry date at the front, and put newly received batches behind them, so that the stock closest to expiry is always dispensed or sold first.
