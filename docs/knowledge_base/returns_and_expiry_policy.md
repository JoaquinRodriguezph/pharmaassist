# Returns And Expiry Policy

Document ID: POL-RET-001. Applies to: all branches.

## Purpose And Scope

This policy explains how counter staff handle products that customers bring back, and how the pharmacy manages stock that is close to or past its expiry date. It applies to every branch and to every staff member who serves customers, receives deliveries or checks shelves. The aim is to protect patient safety, keep every product that reaches a customer beyond doubt, and give staff one consistent answer when a customer or colleague asks what to do. Where this policy and an instruction from the supervising pharmacist differ, follow the pharmacist and tell the branch manager so that the policy can be corrected.

## Customer Returns

Return decisions are made at the counter and apply in the same way at every branch.

1. Prescription medications cannot be returned or refunded once they have left the pharmacy counter. This includes all antibiotics, such as amoxicillin, even when the box is unopened and the customer has the receipt.
2. A dispensed prescription item is eligible for replacement only when it is damaged or is covered by a manufacturer recall.
3. Unopened over-the-counter (OTC) items and vitamins with an intact tamper seal and the original receipt may be returned or exchanged within 7 calendar days of the purchase date.
4. Cold-chain items and prescription drugs are excluded from this return period.

If a return cannot be accepted, explain the reason politely, and offer to ask the supervising pharmacist to speak with the customer.

## Expired Medications

Expiry checks are carried out at every branch as part of the monthly stock check.

- Any stock within 90 days of its expiration date must be pulled from the active sales shelves during the monthly check.
- Items that are within 30 days of expiry, or that have already expired, must be logged by batch number in the expiry log and moved to the labeled quarantine bin in the backroom.
- Quarantined stock stays in the bin until it is collected for supplier return or sent for disposal.
- Expired products must never be dispensed or sold, and must not be moved back to the sales floor.

Copy the batch number from the outer box. If the box is damaged and the number cannot be read, record the product name and expiry date, and tell the supervising pharmacist.
