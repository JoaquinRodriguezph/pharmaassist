# Paracetamol 500 mg Tablets: Product Leaflet

## About This Medicine

Paracetamol 500 mg tablets are used for the relief of mild to moderate pain, such as headache, toothache, muscle aches and period pain, and for the reduction of fever. Each tablet contains 500 mg of paracetamol as the active ingredient. This leaflet describes the use of the adult 500 mg tablet only. Keep the tablets in their original packaging, store them at room temperature away from heat and moisture, and keep them out of sight and reach of children. Do not use the tablets after the expiry date printed on the pack.

## Dosage And Administration

Adults: take 1 to 2 tablets (500 mg to 1000 mg) every 4 to 6 hours when needed. Leave at least 4 hours between doses.

Do not take more than 4000 mg (8 tablets of 500 mg) in any 24-hour period. Swallow the tablets whole with water. Taking more than the stated dose will not give faster or better relief, and can be dangerous.

If pain or fever continues, or gets worse, speak to a pharmacist or doctor.

This leaflet covers adult 500 mg tablets only. It does not give doses for children, or for liquid or other forms of paracetamol. Dosing for children depends on age and weight, and must be calculated by a pharmacist or physician.

## Warnings And Precautions

Do not take paracetamol together with any other medicine that contains paracetamol, including cold and flu remedies. Taking more than one paracetamol-containing product can cause an accidental overdose and severe liver damage (hepatotoxicity). Check the ingredients on the label of every cold or flu product before use.

Chronic heavy alcohol use increases the risk of liver toxicity. If you drink alcohol regularly and heavily, consult a doctor or pharmacist before taking paracetamol.

Seek urgent medical help immediately if you think too many tablets have been taken, even if you feel well, because liver damage can develop without early symptoms.
